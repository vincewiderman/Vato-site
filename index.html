09 / FAQQuick answers

Before you
inquire.

What does open-format mean?+

I can move across hip-hop, house, pop, throwbacks, country, and other styles. Transitions and pacing are mixed live.

Does VATO provide sound and microphones?+

Professional sound and microphone support are available. The exact system is confirmed from the venue, guest count, coverage, and event requirements.

Can we give must-play and do-not-play songs?+

Yes. We'll discuss both before the event, and I'll mix around those priorities.

Does VATO travel?+

Yes, selectively. VATO is based in Morgantown and prioritizes West Virginia. The completed Mebane wedding is North Carolina experience, not an established North Carolina office.
