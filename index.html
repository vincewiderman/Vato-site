What does open-format mean?+

The set can move across hip-hop, house, pop, throwbacks, country, and other styles as the room calls for it. Transitions and pacing are mixed live.

Does VATO provide sound and microphones?+

Professional sound and microphone support are available. The exact system is confirmed from the venue, guest count, coverage, and event requirements.

Can we give must-play and do-not-play songs?+

Yes. Client priorities are discussed ahead of time, then VATO reads the room around them so the plan and live energy work together.

Does VATO travel?+

Yes, selectively. VATO is based in Morgantown and prioritizes West Virginia. The completed Mebane wedding is North Carolina experience, not an established North Carolina office.

Send the event basics below. VATO will confirm availability, clarify production and coverage, and provide a specific quote. An inquiry alone does not reserve the date.
